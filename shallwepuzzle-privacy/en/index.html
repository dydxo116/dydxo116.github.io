2. Game saves and information on your device

The app checks the date of birth in device memory at first use to determine whether the user belongs to the protected or adult category. The date of birth and numeric age are not saved or sent to a server. Only the broad audience category and optional analytics/crash-reporting choice are kept in device storage excluded from Android backup. Skipping the date or missing/corrupt settings results in the protected category.

Stage progress, stars, high scores, unfinished games, recently played games, sound, vibration and language preferences, tutorial status, advertising intervals and consent choices are saved in the app’s or SDK’s local device storage. We do not offer a game account or a separate server that uploads and synchronizes your game-save files.

Google Play Games backup is optional and separate from advertising age protection. The SDK starts only after you read the connection notice and choose to connect. When Google sign-in and storage access are allowed, stages, stars and best records are saved in Google Saved Games and merged across devices. Birth dates, ad choices, settings and unfinished boards are not included in this backup. Google also processes the Play Games profile and the SDK’s own usage and diagnostic data to provide and improve the service. This app does not request friends, email or social features, or use Play Games data for advertising. Supervised child accounts depend on Google’s parental approval and service support; storage is not guaranteed for every account. All games remain playable with local saves if you do not connect or access is denied. Protected advertising and Firebase settings remain in effect. The backup choice is kept in device storage excluded from Android backup. Existing users also see the notice once, and connection is available later in app Settings.

The app does not require registration or sign-in. It does not ask you to submit your name, telephone number, address, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or precise GPS location, or include features to collect that information. If you email us, we receive your sender email address and message.

If Android backup and restore is enabled, some app data may be backed up by the operating-system provider and restored after reinstallation, depending on your device and Google account settings. This is separate from a developer-operated game account. You can manage it in Android backup settings.

An update-specific installation identifier and authentication token, app version, language, operating system, and the IP address used for the connection are processed to deliver configuration and operate this feature. This is separate from optional analytics and crash reporting; checking for updates does not enable Analytics, Crashlytics, or advertising ID collection. We do not use advertising or analytics user properties, experiments, or personalization for these checks.

5. Advertising choices, consent and grounds for processing

The app waits for a local age decision or a choice to skip before making advertising-privacy or ad requests. Optional adult analytics/crash-reporting consent is unchecked by default, and declining does not restrict any game. Turning off Usage analytics and crash reporting in Settings stops future collection and deletes unsent crash reports on the device. It does not automatically erase records already transmitted.

The advertising SDK requests ads after Google UMP indicates that ad requests are permitted. Advertising notices or consent messages may appear depending on your region and Google’s requirements. When privacy options are required, you can revisit or change choices through the advertising privacy options entry in app settings. This entry is displayed when UMP determines that it is required.

The app requests G-rated, non-personalized ads. For protected users under 18 or of unknown age, it applies Google child-age treatment and the UMP under-age-of-consent setting. Non-personalized or limited ads may still process IP/general location, permitted identifiers, interactions and diagnostics. Declining advertising consent or an ad load failure does not prevent play.

You can manage available advertising-ID choices, such as deleting the ID, in Android privacy/advertising settings. Names and options vary by device. Google account advertising controls apply separately within the scope of Google’s services.

Depending on applicable law, processing relies on providing the service, legitimate interests in service reliability, security and improvement, legal obligations, or consent where required. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Applicable consent requirements must be met in regions that require them. Advertising consent and analytics/diagnostics processing have separate scopes.

9. Children and minors

The app is for all ages. The app applies conservative protections to users under 18 and those who do not enter a date of birth. Birth dates are used only for the local age decision, not saved or transmitted. All games remain available to every age. An adult age is not preselected or presented as a condition for game benefits.

Protected users do not start Firebase analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Ads use Google Mobile Ads within the Families self-certified version range, with G-rated, non-personalized settings and child-directed ad treatment. Protected users’ Android advertising IDs are not transmitted. Child-directed ad treatment does not remove all network processing needed to serve contextual ads and maintain security; see the SDK information above.

Applicable rules on children’s information, parental permission and additional protections still apply. Parents or guardians concerned about a child’s information or processing without required permission can contact our privacy email. We will investigate where reasonably possible and take necessary deletion, restriction or other measures.

Do not include passwords, government identifiers, payment details or other sensitive information that is unnecessary to resolve a support request.
